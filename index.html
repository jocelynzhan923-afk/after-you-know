ABOUT THE PROJECT

This experience explores how authorship and context shape creative value. Your selections and display decisions form a small exhibition.

Responses stay in this page and are not sent to a server. Three of the four images use AI, so choosing an AI image alone does not demonstrate a preference for AI.

Source information

The project owner supplied the images and their classifications. Creator credits, tools, prompts and editing histories remain undocumented. A camera photograph may also involve editing. This small, uneven sample cannot establish a general bias.
