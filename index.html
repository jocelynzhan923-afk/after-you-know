ABOUT THE PROJECT

This small experiment explores how authorship and context shape creative value. The pairings invite reflection, rather than a general conclusion about human or AI creativity.

Image sources & project context +

The project owner supplied these four images and their source classifications. Both temple images and fashion image A are identified as AI-generated. Fashion image B is identified as a camera photograph. Photographer credits, generation tools, prompts, and editing histories have not yet been documented.

These samples differ in style, framing, and resolution. A preference change can have several causes. This website uses “camera photograph” as a source label, without implying an absence of editing or AI assistance.

The project builds on Professor Jung Choi’s feedback that value may depend on context, audience, and spectatorship.
